THE STORY OF CANNES

Founded in 1946, the Cannes Film Festival was created as a celebration of international cinema and a meeting point for filmmakers from around the world. Over the decades, Cannes has become a landmark event in the film industry, bringing together directors, actors, artists and audiences to discover new stories and celebrate the art of filmmaking. From groundbreaking films and emerging voices to legendary premieres and unforgettable moments on the red carpet, the festival has become part of cinema history. Every year, Cannes continues this tradition while giving a platform to new perspectives and shaping the future of cinema.
