BEST DIRECTION

La bola negra

Javier Calvo and Javier Ambrossi received the Best Director Award, The award was presented for La bola negra, their first feature film selected in the Official Competition, where it competed alongside 21 other films for the festival’s main prizes. The film tells three interconnected stories set in different periods of Spanish history, exploring themes of desire, identity, silence and inheritance, with Federico García Lorca as one of its central references.

Fjord

Fjord, directed by Romanian filmmaker Cristian Mungiu, received the Palme d’Or, the highest award of the Cannes Film Festival. The film was part of the Official Competition, selected among 22 feature films competing for the festival’s main prize. The award was presented during the closing ceremony of the 79th edition, placing Fjord at the centre of this year’s Cannes celebrations.

PALME D'ORE

GRAN PRIX

Minotaure

Russian filmmaker Andreï Zviaguintsev received the Grand Prix for Minotaur, one of the highest distinctions awarded by the Cannes jury. The film competed in the Official Competition alongside 21 other titles and was recognised during the closing ceremony of the 79th Festival. Zviaguintsev also spoke about the long development of the screenplay, which took around a year and a half to write.
